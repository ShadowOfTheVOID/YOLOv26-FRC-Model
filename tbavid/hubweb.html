2Where to count

Outline around the hub's opening — recommended. Put its top edge 3-4 ball widths above the hood, where a falling ball is still clear of the hood's mesh.
Or an exit line — untested: balls pile up at the exit and stop crossing one by one. Only for a camera aimed right at the chute, and check it with 20 balls.

Blends a trained model's count with the colour count. On four Einstein matches: 6.8% error against 10.1% for colour alone; on the Central Valley broadcast it was worse (34.5%), so check it on your camera. Fast enough on Apple silicon or an NVIDIA GPU; on a slower computer it turns itself off after about 10 s and counts by colour.
